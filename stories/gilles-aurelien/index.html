His story

Beyond the stat sheet.

“I know it's not gonna be easy. But I'm not gonna stop here.”

Where it started

Gilles Aurelien grew up in Bracknell, England, with a football at his feet and a father who believed in him before anyone else did. At seven, a scout invited him to trial at Southampton FC. He spent eight-plus years in the academy, traveling across Europe and winning Player of the Tournament in Spain.

The setback

COVID wiped out his post-academy trials. He and his dad chose America. He started at Otero Junior College, helped the program reach nationals for the first time in 12 years, then tore his ACL nine games into his sophomore season. His father flew from the UK for the surgery.

College

He came back. He transferred to Utah Valley University, became one of the best midfielders in the WAC, and graduated with a BS degree.

What comes next

After graduating, he returned to pursue professional opportunities. He was listed for the MLS SuperDraft. He has played against pros. He is not done.
