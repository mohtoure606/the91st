His story

Beyond the stat sheet.

“Your ceiling is someone else's floor. Keep climbing.”

Where it started

Elijah Tanger was born in Nigeria, where football is not a hobby, it is life. From four years old, his father was his first coach. At twelve, his family immigrated to Canada.

The academy years

He earned a spot at Toronto FC Academy at U14, continued at the De Rosario Academy from U16 to U18, and trained at Phoenix Football Academy in Abuja.

The setback

COVID nearly closed the window. When it reopened, he earned a spot among the top sixty players across Canada.

Breaking through

In 2024 he trained with Real Monarchs of MLS Next Pro, won Player of the Year in the UPSL Mountain Division, and made the USL Academy All-Tournament Team. He was top scorer again in 2025.

What comes next

He is the first person in his family to go to college, and he is still chasing a professional contract.
