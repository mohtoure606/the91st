His story.

Creative midfielder and set-piece threat, developed as an attacking midfielder at the Real Salt Lake Academy. Four-time All-WAC at Utah Valley, a program record.

Alejandro Silva was born and raised in Salt Lake City, the son of Elsa and José Luis Silva and one of four brothers. He learned the game in the city's Hispanic Sunday leagues, with his dad as his first teacher, and played club soccer for La Roca, winning a 2018 Utah Youth Soccer Association state title.

Read his full story

The academy

He came up through the Real Salt Lake Academy as an attacking midfielder for the U-19 team. When the academy first offered him a spot, he admits he was scared to take it. He took it anyway. He trained with the Real Monarchs and started in their preseason, then heard the words every academy player fears: they would not sign him.

Utah Valley

He turned that rejection into one of the best careers in Utah Valley history. He started every match he played across four seasons (56 starts), earned All-WAC honours a program-record four times, including First Team in 2022 and 2023, and became the 11th player in program history to pass 4,000 minutes. As a senior he led the team in goals, and his strike against Pacific was named WAC Top Play.

Oregon State

He finished his college career as a graduate transfer at Oregon State. It was a step up, and also, in his words, one of the lowest moments of his career. He talks openly about reaching out for help, and about the person who helped him through it.

After college

Since college he has kept playing, including a spell with Utah United. He describes life after college as harder than college itself, because the system that structured every day is gone, and he is still chasing the professional game.
