His story.

Direct, 6'2" forward from Ghana's Phoenix Academy, with NCAA Division I experience at Utah Valley. Now scoring for Salt Lake Community College, the 2026 Region 18 champions.

Sharif Sayawu grew up in Agona Duakwa, a small town in Ghana where football is not something you choose, it is something that chooses you. His parents, Iddrisu Sayawu and Ayishatu Mohammed, believed in what they saw and sacrificed to make sure he had the chance to pursue it.

Read his full story

The academy

He earned his way to Phoenix Academy, one of Ghana's most respected development programs, which has sent players to MLS, USL, and NCAA Division I. He repaid their belief with 15 goals in 2023 and Best Player honors at the 2024 Showcase. The calls from America came. He chose Utah Valley University.

College

Getting on that plane was one of the hardest things he has ever done. He left his family, his country, and everything familiar, and arrived in Utah as a freshman. In his very first collegiate game he recorded two assists. Weeks later he scored his first Division I goal.

What comes next

After a season at Utah Valley, Sharif transferred to Salt Lake Community College, where he is a sophomore forward in 2026. He is still carrying the hopes of a family back in Ghana, and his story is just beginning.
