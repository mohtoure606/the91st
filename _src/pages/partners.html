Partnerships

Better opportunities happen together.

We believe the transition beyond college soccer requires more than individual effort. It takes clubs, organizations, educators, mentors, and people willing to create opportunities.

The 91st Foundation works to connect these resources and build pathways that help college soccer players navigate what comes next.

Why partner with The 91st?

A practical way to back players at a turning point.

We're a young, focused organization. We do the groundwork with players, get to know their stories, and connect them with partners who are a real fit.

Reach motivated college soccer athletes

Meet players who have competed at the college level and are actively deciding what's next.

Help build football pathways

Help create routes into domestic and international football for players who are ready for the next level.

Support further education

Open doors to graduate programs, scholarships, and study options for athletes who want to keep learning.

Contribute to athlete transition

Be part of programs that look after the whole person: health, wellbeing, and career.

Join a growing international network

Connect with clubs, schools, and organizations across countries that share the same goal.

Make a tangible difference

Your involvement can change what the next few years look like for a real student-athlete.

Partnership opportunities

Five ways to work with us.

Every partnership is shaped around what your organization does well. These are starting points, not fixed packages.

01

Football clubs and development programs

Professional and semi-professional clubs, academies, player development programs, sports management companies, agents, and scouting networks.

What you can offer
Trials, training periods, roster opportunities, scouting access, or development environments.
What collaboration could look like
We share profiles of players who fit your needs, arrange introductions, and support players through the process.
How it benefits players
A real, vetted route to be seen by people who make football decisions, at home or abroad.
How to start
Tell us the positions, levels, and timelines you're interested in. Start an inquiry.
02

Universities and educational institutions

Universities and colleges, graduate programs, scholarship organizations, and international education programs.

What you can offer
Graduate places, scholarships, academic programs, or opportunities to study and play.
What collaboration could look like
We share your programs with players who are a fit and help them understand admissions and next steps.
How it benefits players
A clear path to keep learning, often while staying connected to the game.
How to start
Share the programs and eligibility details you'd like players to know about. Start an inquiry.
03

Health and athlete support organizations

Sports medicine providers, physical therapy clinics, mental health resources, and athlete transition organizations.

What you can offer
Recovery services, mental health support, assessments, workshops, or referral pathways.
What collaboration could look like
A verified listing in our resource directory, referrals for players who need support, or joint sessions on transition.
How it benefits players
Trusted care for the body and mind during one of the hardest moments of an athletic career.
How to start
Tell us about your services, locations, and who you serve. Start an inquiry.
04

Career and professional development organizations

Employers, career development organizations, and professional mentorship programs.

What you can offer
Mentors, internships, job openings, career workshops, or networking events.
What collaboration could look like
Mentorship matches, introductions to roles that suit former athletes, or sessions on translating sport into career skills.
How it benefits players
A head start on a career beyond soccer, with people who value what athletes bring.
How to start
Let us know what roles, mentors, or programs you can offer. Start an inquiry.
05

Sponsors, foundations, and community partners

Corporate sponsors, foundations, philanthropic organizations, individual donors, and community partners.

What you can offer
Funding, in-kind support, equipment, event space, or your platform and network.
What collaboration could look like
Support for specific programs, player story production, or the foundation's general operations.
How it benefits players
Your support helps us reach more players and build the programs they need.
How to start
Tell us what you'd like to support, or make a gift online. Start an inquiry.

How partnership works

Simple, personal, and practical.

  1. Connect

    Introduce your organization and what you're interested in.

  2. Explore

    We talk through shared goals and what opportunities make sense.

  3. Collaborate

    We agree on a practical way of working together that suits both sides.

  4. Create impact

    We connect players with the opportunities and resources you offer.
