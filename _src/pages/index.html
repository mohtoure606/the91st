The challenge

The game doesn't have to end here.

College soccer players give years to the sport: early mornings, travel, injuries, and the discipline of balancing a degree with a season. Then graduation comes, and the structure that held everything together is gone.

  1. 01

    Few professional spots, fewer open doors

    Talented players go unseen because they lack exposure and connections, not ability.

  2. 02

    International football feels out of reach

    Clubs abroad can be a real option, but most players don't know who to contact or how the process works.

  3. 03

    Money and education get in the way

    Finishing a degree, starting a graduate program, or paying for a trial can all stall a player's plans.

  4. 04

    Careers outside sport start from scratch

    Athletes have discipline, teamwork, and resilience, but often no network to help them turn that into a career.

  5. 05

    Too many face it alone

    When soccer has been your identity, losing it is hard. Guidance and community make the difference.

Our mission

Supporting the player beyond the player.

Performance on the pitch is one part of a person. We focus on the whole athlete: their football ambitions, their education, their health, and the career and community that will carry them forward.

The pathways

Where can your next chapter take you?

Every player's journey is different. Some keep chasing the professional game. Others go back to the classroom or build a career off the pitch. Most do a bit of both.

Route01

Professional football, in the United States and internationally

Visibility with clubs, academies, and development programs that are looking for players.

Route02

Further education and graduate study

Graduate programs, academic opportunities, and scholarships that keep doors open.

Route03

Career development and employment

Mentorship and connections that help athletes turn what they learned in sport into a profession.

Route04

Health, wellbeing, and personal growth

Support for recovery, mental health, and finding purpose after competitive sport.

Our promise is support, not guarantees. We work hard to open doors and make introductions, but we don't promise contracts, placements, admissions, or outcomes.
