Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information The 91st Foundation ("The 91st," "we," "us") collects through the91st.org, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email team@the91st.org.

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on this site (player inquiry, partnership inquiry, get-involved, or resource suggestion), we collect the information you enter, such as your name, email address, school or organization, and your message. Please don't include sensitive personal information, such as medical details or government ID numbers, in a form.

How we use it

  • To read and respond to your inquiry.
  • To explore opportunities on your behalf when you ask us to, for example by sharing a player's profile with a club. We will ask before sharing your personal contact details with a third party.
  • To run and improve the foundation's programs.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

  • Formspree receives and delivers the forms on this site to our email.
  • Donorbox processes donations. Payment details are handled by Donorbox and its payment processors, not stored by us.
  • YouTube hosts our videos. Videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for security.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party services you choose to use, such as a YouTube video or the Donorbox donation page, may set their own cookies.

Player stories

Player stories, photos, and videos are published with the participation of the players featured. If you are featured and want something changed or removed, email team@the91st.org.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep inquiry information only as long as we need it to respond and to follow up on opportunities. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing team@the91st.org.

Children

This site is intended for college-age players and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the foundation grows. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
