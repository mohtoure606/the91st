Our story

Built from love for the game.

I grew up in Mali, West Africa, where soccer was not a hobby. It was life. Every day, from the moment school ended until the sun went down, I was on the pitch. Soccer was how I understood the world, how I built friendships, and how I dreamed.

When I moved to Utah to attend Utah Valley University, I carried that love with me. I tried to play at the college level, but a series of torn ACLs took that away before it ever really started. Standing on the sideline, I had a choice. I chose to stay. I committed to earning my Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering and found new purpose in helping the people around me.

But what I could not stop watching, year after year, were my friends. Talented, hardworking athletes who lit up that field. And then graduation came, and the lights went out. No agent. No plan. No roadmap. Everything they had built their identity around simply vanished, and the world moved on like it never mattered.

It mattered.

The 91st exists because of those players. I am not a former pro. I am someone who came from across the world with a dream, watched people I love struggle in silence, and decided that was not acceptable. Every player deserves a next chapter. This foundation is how we help them write it.

Mohamed Toure, Founder

Why "The 91st"

A match lasts 90 minutes. Life doesn't.

The 91st minute is what comes after regulation time, when the game you trained for is over and what happens next is still being decided. That's where we work: in the moment after the final whistle, helping players figure out the next chapter.

The problem

Years of commitment, then a sudden stop.

College soccer players invest years of time, discipline, and personal sacrifice in the game, often while earning a degree and, for many international players, far from home.

When their college careers end, many face real uncertainty. Some still want to play professionally. Others want to keep studying, start a career, or find direction outside the sport. Most don't have the connections, resources, or guidance to make that move with confidence.

Opportunities exist, in clubs at home and abroad, in graduate programs, in companies that value what athletes bring. The gap is access. The 91st exists to help close it.

Mission and vision

Mission

We support college soccer players as they transition beyond competitive soccer by connecting them with opportunities in professional football, education, career development, and wellbeing through a growing network of clubs, organizations, mentors, and resources.

Vision

A world where no college soccer player faces life after the game alone, and every athlete has access to the opportunities and support needed to build a meaningful future.

Our values

What guides the work.

  • Opportunity

    Talent and effort should be matched by a fair shot. We work to open doors that are usually closed to players without connections.

  • Community

    The transition is easier with people who understand it. We're building a network of players, mentors, and partners who look out for each other.

  • Integrity

    We are honest with players and partners about what we can and can't do. We don't overpromise, and we don't exaggerate.

  • Development

    Growth doesn't stop at graduation. We support players as athletes, students, professionals, and people.

  • Inclusion

    We support players of every background, nationality, and division, whether they are chasing the pro game or stepping away from it.

  • Purpose

    A player's journey isn't defined by how long they play, but by what they build next.

Who we serve

College soccer players at a turning point.

  1. 01

    Seniors and recent graduates

    Players finishing their college careers who want to keep playing, keep studying, or start something new.

  2. 02

    Players pursuing the professional game

    Athletes looking for visibility with clubs, academies, and development programs in the United States and internationally.

  3. 03

    International student-athletes

    Players who came to the US to play and study, and who face extra decisions about where their future lies.

  4. 04

    Players stepping away from the sport

    Athletes building a life and career beyond competitive soccer, who need guidance, community, and a plan.

Why it matters

Life after college soccer deserves attention.

A lot of support exists to help young players reach college soccer. Much less exists for what happens when it ends. For many athletes, the end of their college career is also the end of a daily routine, a team, and an identity they have built since childhood.

Handled well, that moment can be the start of something meaningful: a professional contract, a graduate degree, a career, a healthier relationship with the game. Handled alone, it can mean years of drifting. We want more players to have the first experience.

The organization

Who we are.

The 91st is a young, founder-led nonprofit. We are building our team, our advisors, and our partner network step by step, and we're open about where we are in that journey.
